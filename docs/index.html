    How to read this

    • Each indicator is scored green, yellow or red against fixed thresholds (shown on each card).
    • Indicators are grouped into six areas. An area turns red when most of its signals are red.
    • One red signal is noise. A cluster is the warning. The overall verdict moves to Warning when two or more areas are red at once, and to High risk at four or more.
    • This follows Ray Dalio's debt-cycle framework: deleveraging shows up as tighter credit, rising debt-service burdens, slowing credit growth relative to income and broadening delinquencies, and is then confirmed by weaker spending and jobs.
    • This is an educational tool, not investment advice.

    Data: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. This product uses the FRED® API but is not endorsed or certified by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
